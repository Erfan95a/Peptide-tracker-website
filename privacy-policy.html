Privacy Policy for Peptide Tracker

Short version: Peptide Tracker is a personal tracking and education app. We use the data you enter to provide dose logs, body logs, schedules, reminders, exports, and subscription access. We do not sell your personal data. Health Connect and Apple Health access is optional and controlled by you.

This Privacy Policy explains how Peptide Tracker Calculator App ("Peptide Tracker", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application. The app is intended for personal record-keeping, research, and educational purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Information We Collect

Account information

When you sign in, we collect account information provided through the authentication provider you choose, such as your user identifier and email address. On Android, the app supports Google Sign-In through Supabase Auth. On iOS, the app supports Apple Sign-In through Supabase Auth.

Information you enter in the app

The app lets you create and manage personal tracking records. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • Peptide names, custom peptide settings, units, half-life estimates, peak-time estimates, interval settings, inventory details, and notes.
  • Dose logs, amounts, units, routes, injection sites, notes, timestamps, and schedule information.
  • Observation and symptom logs, including type, label, category, severity, notes, related peptide name, and timestamps.
  • Body logs, including weight, body fat, waist, hip, chest, abdomen, neck, arm, thigh, resting heart rate, blood pressure, units, notes, and timestamps.
  • Feedback, feature requests, ratings, and related message text that you choose to send.
  • Legal disclaimer acceptance records, including the disclaimer version, acceptance time, source, account identifier, and email address.

Subscription information

If you purchase or restore a subscription, RevenueCat processes subscription events and sends subscription status to our backend so the app can unlock paid features. We store your app user identifier, subscription plan, and update time. Payment details are handled by the applicable app store and RevenueCat; we do not receive your full payment card information.

Notifications and device settings

If you enable reminders, the app stores notification preferences and schedule information so local device notifications can be scheduled. You can disable reminders in the app or through your device settings.

Health Connect and Apple Health Data

Health data access is optional. The app asks for health permissions only when you choose to connect Health Connect on Android or Apple Health on iOS. You can deny, limit, or revoke these permissions at any time in your device health settings.

Health data we may read

If you grant permission, Peptide Tracker may read selected wellness data to show context next to your personal tracking records. This may include:

  • Steps and step trends.
  • Sleep sessions, sleep duration, and sleep stages.
  • Exercise or workout sessions and workout duration.
  • Resting heart rate and heart rate variability.
  • Active calories burned, exercise minutes, VO2 max, and heart rate recovery where available.
  • Respiratory rate.
  • Weight, body fat percentage, and lean body mass.

Health data we may write

If you explicitly choose to share a body log to Health Connect or Apple Health, Peptide Tracker may write eligible values you entered, such as weight, body fat percentage, resting heart rate, and blood pressure. Sharing is optional and occurs only when you turn on the sharing option for that entry or use a health-sharing action.

How health data is used

Health data is used to display wellness context, charts, trends, and exports inside the app, and to write eligible values back to Health Connect or Apple Health only when you choose to do so. We do not use Health Connect or Apple Health data for advertising, credit decisions, employment decisions, insurance eligibility, or sale to data brokers.

How health data is stored

Health metrics read directly from Health Connect or Apple Health are stored on your device for app display and syncing status. Manually entered body logs are saved in your Peptide Tracker account and may be stored in Supabase so you can access them across devices. These body logs can include health-related values that you typed into the app.

Your access and deletion controls for health data

  • You can view, edit, export, or delete your body logs and other tracking entries in the app.
  • You can revoke Health Connect permissions in Android Health Connect settings.
  • You can revoke Apple Health permissions in iOS Health settings.
  • You can delete your Peptide Tracker account in the app under Settings, Data & Account, Delete account.
  • You can request access, correction, export, or deletion by contacting us at e.ahmadi95@protonmail.com.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide dose tracking, timeline, schedules, reminders, inventory, observations, body logs, health summaries, and exports.
  • Sync your app data across devices when you are signed in.
  • Provide and verify subscription access.
  • Respond to feedback, support requests, and feature requests.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and improve app reliability.
  • Record required legal disclaimer acceptance.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce app terms.

Where Information Is Stored

Some app preferences and cached data are stored locally on your device using app storage. Account data and synced records are stored using Supabase. Subscription status is processed through RevenueCat and may be mirrored to Supabase. Notification scheduling is handled locally through your device notification system.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only as needed to operate the app through the providers listed above, comply with law, protect rights and safety, prevent fraud or abuse, or complete a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition. We do not share Health Connect or Apple Health data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention

We keep account and app records while your account is active or as needed to provide the app. You can delete individual entries in the app where supported. If you delete your account, account-linked records are deleted or anonymized where our database rules and legal obligations allow. Some records, such as legal consent logs, support records, backup copies, or records required for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, tax, accounting, or legal compliance, may be retained for a limited period.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can access and update many records directly in the app.
  • You can export your data from the app where export features are available.
  • You can delete doses, body logs, observations, schedules, and inventory items in the app where supported.
  • You can delete your account in Settings under Data & Account.
  • You can revoke health permissions in Health Connect or Apple Health settings.
  • You can contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or portability of your personal data.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including authenticated access, database access controls, and provider-managed security features. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Peptide Tracker is intended for adults 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

International Users

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. Data protection laws may differ between countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate legal bases and safeguards for processing and transfers.
